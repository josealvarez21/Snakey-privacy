Privacy Policy for Snakey

Last updated: 9.9.2026

In short: Snakey does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The only data the app keeps — your best score and which skins you've unlocked — stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Information we collect

Snakey does not collect any personal information. There's no account creation, and the app never asks for your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other identifying information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device only, using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults):

  • Your best score
  • Which cosmetic skins you've unlocked

This information never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and is deleted automatically if you delete the app.

Third-party services

Snakey does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services, and the app does not display ads.

Sharing your score

If you tap "Share score," the app hands an image to Apple's standard share sheet. Whatever you choose to do from there — for example, sending it in Messages or posting it to a social app — is between you and the destination you pick. Snakey itself does not transmit that image anywhere on its own.

Children's privacy

Snakey does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information at all, this applies equally to players of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change — for example, if a future update adds a leaderboard or analytics — this page will be updated to reflect that, along with a new "Last updated" date above.
